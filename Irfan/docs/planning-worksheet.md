## Exit check (after research)
Go back to this page after the research checklist. Stop looping when all are true:
- [ ] The problem is one sentence.
- [ ] One real person with the problem said "yes, that's me". (Ask about their last time it happened, not whether they would use your idea.)
- [ ] I know my riskiest assumption and one cheap way to test it (a day or less).
- [ ] Version 1 fits my time box, for example one weekend.

Loop at most 3 rounds. Still not ready? Pick a smaller idea.
