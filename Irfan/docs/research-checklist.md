# Research checklist

Do this after the planning worksheet. Find at least three existing solutions to your problem.
Apply it to each, then write what you learned back into the worksheet.

For each solution:

1. **Who built it, and can they be wrong in public?** (a company, a student, an abandoned repo)
2. **What does it cost?** Free, free with limits, paid. Read the limits.
3. **What does it do well?** Name one thing worth copying.
4. **What does it miss?** Features, users, languages, platforms it ignores.
5. **What do users complain about?** Read reviews, issues and forum threads, not the landing page.
6. **Is the information dated?** Check last update and release date. Old advice and dead projects mislead.
7. **Did a second source say the same thing?** One review is an opinion. Two is a pattern.
8. **Fact or guess?** Separate what you verified ("last release was in 2024") from what you assume ("probably unmaintained").

## Write-up (3 lines)
- What already exists: ______________________
- The gap I found: ______________________
- What this changes in my plan: ______________________

## Rules
- Do not copy another project's code or text; learn from it.
- If you cannot find any competitor, check you searched the right words before concluding the field is empty.
